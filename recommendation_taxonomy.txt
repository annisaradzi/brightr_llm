FINDINGS RECOMMENDATION CODES (select exactly one primary recommendationCode):

TBR � To Be Repaired
Permanent repair per code/standard. Methods include replacement (sectional/component/item),
weld overlay, insert plate/patch. Refer PTS or ASME PCC2.

TBRy � To Be Rectified
Acceptable rectification other than TBR; usually non-pressure-part findings.
Examples: re-tighten loose bolts/nuts; install missing PV nameplate; provide insulation inspection window.

TBP � To Be Painted
Painting per PTS 15.20.03 or equivalent: maintenance/spot/touch-up, total recoating during TSM,
approved alternatives (e.g. Rust Stabilizer).

TBM � To Be Monitored
For internal/RL-related findings: monitor thickness via spot UTTG, UT scanning/corrosion mapping, or profile RT.

TBS � To Strip Off (Insulation)
Remove insulation for visual inspection or suitable NDT; assess CUI (may lead to TBR/TBP).

Rust grades (ISO 8501 visual): Ri1, Ri2, R3, R4, R5
